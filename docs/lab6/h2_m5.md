# H2 — Individual reflection (M5 / fumeneko)

RAG works like an open-book exam: the assistant searches its reference documents before answering instead of relying only on memory.
The retriever ranks document chunks that may contain evidence relevant to the question.
For this evaluation task, I added paraphrased questions about thesis registration, library borrowing and student-card replacement.
I also added out-of-scope and privacy cases to check that the assistant abstains instead of inventing information or exposing another student's record.
The expected source IDs were checked against the knowledge-base headings, and the final comparison should be rerun after A1 and A2 are integrated.
